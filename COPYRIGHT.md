## Standard quotation permission

The Way Version text may be quoted in print, digital, visual, and audio works without advance written permission when **all** of the following conditions are met:

1. No more than 500 verses are quoted in total in a single work, product, edition, recording, website, or substantially related series.
2. The quoted verses do not amount to more than one-half of any single book of the Bible.
3. The quoted verses make up less than 25% of the total text or spoken content of the work in which they appear.
4. The Way Version wording is reproduced accurately. Normal typography, capitalization at the beginning of a quotation, and clearly marked omissions are permitted, but the meaning may not be altered or presented misleadingly.
5. The required attribution appears in a reasonably prominent location.
6. The use does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation with The Way Partners LLC.
7. The use is a quotation within a larger work—not publication of a standalone Bible, Scripture portion, competing edition, or product whose principal purpose is to reproduce or substitute for The Way Version.

This standard permission includes ordinary quotation in books and ebooks, articles, websites, blogs, social media, church materials, classroom materials, sermons, presentations, podcasts, videos, broadcasts, and audiobooks, whether the larger work is distributed free of charge or sold.

The 500-verse allowance applies to the total use in the work, not 500 verses per chapter, page, post, episode, format, or Bible book. Closely related editions, installments, posts, episodes, or products may be treated as one work when they function as a single publication or project.

Added emphasis, explanatory brackets, paraphrases, abridgments, or other editorial changes must be identified as the user’s own and must not be represented as part of The Way Version text. Quotations should include sufficient context to avoid materially changing the sense of the passage.
