# Four extra Romans divine referent corrections — 14 October 2026

### Romans 4:21
BEFORE: and being fully assured that what he had promised, he was able also to perform.
AFTER: and being fully assured that what God had promised, God was able also to perform.
REASON: In Romans 4:20–21 the promise was made by God; Avraham is the one assured of it.

### Romans 9:18
BEFORE: So then, he has mercy on whom he desires, and he hardens whom he desires.
AFTER: So then, God has mercy on whom God desires, and God hardens whom God desires.
REASON: Romans 9:15–19 repeatedly names God as the one who has mercy or hardens.

### Romans 15:10
BEFORE: Again he says, "Rejoice, you Gentiles, with his people."
AFTER: Again Scripture says, "Rejoice, you Gentiles, with God’s people."
REASON: Romans 15:9–11 quotation sequence: the scriptural citation has the Gentiles rejoicing with the people of God. Speaker of “Again” is Scripture, not an implicit male God.

### Romans 15:11
BEFORE: Again, "Praise the Master, all you Gentiles! Let all the peoples praise him."
AFTER: Again, "Praise the Master, all you Gentiles! Let all the peoples praise the Master."
REASON: The Master in this cited invitation to praise is God, not Yeshua, so use the established title twice.
