# Luke 2:52 — “the people”

BEFORE: And Yeshua increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with Elohim and people.

AFTER: And Yeshua increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with Elohim and the people.

Rationale: Luke 2:52 uses Greek ἀνθρώποις (anthrōpois), the plural human noun, in contrast to θεῷ (God/Elohim). In the historical social setting it speaks of Yeshua gaining favor with people, not solely men. The Way Version editorial choice “the people” is natural English collective wording for those among whom Yeshua grew and was known. The English definite article is a style/context decision, not a claim that there is a separate Greek article before ἀνθρώποις. This is one exact verse edit, not a global replacement of the word people.
