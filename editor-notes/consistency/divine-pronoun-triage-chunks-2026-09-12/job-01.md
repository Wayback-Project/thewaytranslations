# Job — divine-pronoun triage part 1

Candidates 1–11 of 11. Review-only; no Scripture changes.

- **Job 1:12** — [explicit-divine-subject:he→YHWH] — YHWH said to the Adversary, "Behold, all that he has is in your power. Only on himself don't put forth your hand." So the Adversary went forth from the presence of YHWH.
- **Job 2:3** — [next-sentence-divine-subject:He→God] — YHWH said to the Adversary, "Have you considered my servant Job? For there is none like him in the earth, a blameless and an upright man, one who reveres God, and turns away from harm. He still maintains his integrity, although you incited me against him, to ruin him without cause."
- **Job 2:6** — [explicit-divine-subject:he→YHWH] — YHWH said to the Adversary, "Behold, he is in your hand. Only spare his life."
- **Job 4:9** — [divine-attribute:his→Elohim] — By the breath of Elohim they perish. By the blast of his anger are they consumed.
- **Job 6:9** — [divine-attribute:his→Elohim, explicit-divine-subject:he→Elohim] — even that it would please Elohim to crush me; that he would let loose his hand, and cut me off!
- **Job 20:29** — [explicit-divine-role:him→role-after-pronoun] — This is the portion of a broken person from Elohim, the heritage appointed to him by Elohim."
- **Job 21:15** — [devotion-object:him→Almighty, devotion-object:him→Almighty] — What is the Almighty, that we should serve him? What profit should we have, if we pray to him?'
- **Job 21:19** — [explicit-divine-subject:his→Elohim] — You say, 'Elohim lays up his iniquity for his children.' Let him recompense it to himself, that he may know it.
- **Job 24:22** — [next-sentence-divine-subject:He→Elohim] — Yet Elohim preserves the mighty by Elohim’s power. He rises up who has no assurance of life.
- **Job 33:26** — [explicit-divine-subject:he→Elohim, next-sentence-divine-subject:He→Elohim] — He prays to Elohim, and he is favorable to him, so that he sees his face with joy. He restores to man his righteousness.
- **Job 42:11** — [explicit-divine-role:him→role-after-pronoun] — Then came there to him all his brothers, and all his sisters, and all those who had been of his acquaintance before, and ate bread with him in his house. They comforted him, and consoled him concerning all the harm that YHWH had brought on him. Everyone also gave him a piece of money, and everyone a ring of gold.
