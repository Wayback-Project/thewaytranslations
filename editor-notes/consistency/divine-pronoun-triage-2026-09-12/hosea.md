# Hosea — divine-pronoun residual triage

Heuristic candidates: **8**. Review-only; no Scripture changes.

- **Hosea 1:4** — [divine-attribute:his→YHWH] — YHWH said to him, "Call his name Jezreel; for yet a little while, and I will avenge the blood of Jezreel on the house of Yehu, and will cause the kingdom of the house of Israel to cease.
- **Hosea 3:5** — [divine-attribute:his→YHWH] — Afterward the children of Israel shall return, and seek YHWH their God, and Dawid their king, and shall come with trembling to YHWH and to his blessings in the last days.
- **Hosea 6:1** — [explicit-divine-subject:he→YHWH] — "Come, and let us return to YHWH; for he has torn us to pieces, and he will heal us; he has injured us, and he will bind up our wounds.
- **Hosea 7:10** — [devotion-object:him→God] — The pride of Israel testifies to his face; yet they haven't returned to YHWH their God, nor sought him, for all this.
- **Hosea 11:7** — [explicit-divine-subject:he→Most High] — My people are determined to turn from me. Though they call to the Most High, he certainly won't exalt them.
- **Hosea 11:10** — [explicit-divine-subject:he→YHWH] — They will walk after YHWH, who will roar like a lion; for he will roar, and the children will come trembling from the west.
- **Hosea 12:14** — [explicit-divine-role:him→role-after-pronoun] — Efrayim has bitterly provoked anger. Therefore his blood will be left on him, and his YHWH will repay his contempt.
- **Hosea 13:15** — [explicit-divine-subject:his→YHWH, next-sentence-divine-subject:He→YHWH] — Though he is fruitful among his brothers, an east wind will come, the breath of YHWH coming up from the wilderness; and his spring will become dry, and his fountain will be dried up. He will plunder the storehouse of treasure.
