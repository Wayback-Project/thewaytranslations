# 1 Timothy 6:15 — God / Sovereign royal-title follow-up

BEFORE: which in its own times he will show, who is the blessed and only Ruler, the King of kings, and Master of masters;

AFTER: which God will show at the proper time—God, the blessed and only Ruler, the Sovereign of sovereigns, and Master of masters;

Rationale: Context 1 Timothy 6:13–16: God (not Messiah Yeshua) is the one who reveals Yeshua's appearing at the proper time and is described as the blessed sole Ruler. Recast unmistakable God-referent he as God; following existing Way Version policy replace the divine title King of kings with Sovereign of sovereigns. Preserve the distinct human/messianic King titles elsewhere; render Greek basileus basileōn with explicit translation-equivalent paraphrase, not a claim it literally says Greek sovereign.
