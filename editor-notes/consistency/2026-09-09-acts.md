# Consistency update — Acts

This first pass changes 41 verses in Acts. It is not a completed whole-Bible pronoun review. Other books are unchanged, and some Acts references still require contextual or textual review.

## Editorial basis

The context-first method in [the working specification](../../skill/SKILL.md) and the editor’s consistency instruction guide this pass. Where the divine referent is clear, the existing divine name or title is repeated, or the clause is naturally rephrased. This is an English editorial rendering, not a claim that every source-language pronoun is gender-neutral. Existing divine titles, including Cosmic Parent, remain under their existing editorial policy.

Pronouns referring to Yeshua or human characters are retained. The explicit title “the Master” is retained where the passage uses it, without silently resolving the title to a different person. No automatic masculine-to-feminine or masculine-to-plural substitution is made. Grammatical gender alone does not establish divine biological sex. Acts 8:33 changes reverential capitalization only.

Examples include Acts 1:7 (the Cosmic Parent’s own authority); 7:5 (God’s promise, with Avraham’s human pronouns retained); 8:16 (the Ruach of Elohim); 13:22–23 and 17:31 (existing divine they/their references made explicit); and 17:24–30 (God or the existing title in place of divine masculine pronouns).

The [exact before/after ledger](../../change-logs/new-testament/reports/2026-09-09-acts-consistency.json) records all 41 affected verses. The canonical EPUB and editable Acts source receive identical verse text.

- Continue reviewing Acts and the other 65 books for remaining divine pronouns, including lowercase forms and references crossing verse boundaries. A keyword match is only a candidate, not a confirmed divine referent.
