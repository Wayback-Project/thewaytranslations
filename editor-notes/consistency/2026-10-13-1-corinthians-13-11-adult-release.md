# 1 Corinthians 13:11 — become an adult (canonical + physical print handoff)

BEFORE: When I was a child, I spoke as a child, I felt as a child, I thought as a child. Now that I have become a person, I have put away childish things.

AFTER: When I was a child, I spoke as a child, I felt as a child, I thought as a child. Now that I have become an adult, I have put away childish things.

RATIONALE: The Greek ὅτε γέγονα ἀνήρ (hote gegona anēr) contrasts the state of being a child with reaching adulthood. The speaker did not turn from nonhuman to human. In this rhetorical contrast, adult expresses the relevant maturity without assigning male gender to a generic translation or flattening the intended child/adult contrast. The 1st-person I remains unchanged; gender references to concrete male figures in other Bible verses are unaffected.
