# New Testament divine Father/God masculine-pronoun residual release

**Approved one-release scope:** 45 exact Scripture verses in 12 NT books.

1 Peter 4:19: “entrust their souls to him” → “entrust their souls to God” (faithful Creator).

The 27-book NT candidate audit found many masculine forms that refer to Yeshua or other men. Only unambiguous references to God/YHWH/Elohim/the Cosmic Parent were changed. Textual witnesses and context outrank mechanical matching. Using God versus Elohim versus Cosmic Parent follows the immediately established verse-level divine form; no global rename was authorized.
