# Father -> Cosmic Parent Policy + Divine-Title Boundary

Created: 2026-04-08 UTC
Updated: 2026-09-01 UTC
Status: Active editorial policy

## Active rule
- **Cosmic Parent is reserved for explicit divine Father/parent language in the source or an explicitly documented Father relationship.**
- Do **not** use Cosmic Parent as a generic substitute for `YHWH`, `Elohim`, Greek `theos` (God), or a pronoun whose antecedent is one of those terms.
- Human kinship references remain human kinship language: `father`, `mother`, etc. A human father in a parable is not Cosmic Parent.

## Abba
- Preserve `Abba` where it is actually present in the New Testament source tradition.
- The three NT occurrences are Mark 14:36, Romans 8:15, and Galatians 4:6.
- In each of those passages, `Abba` is followed by Greek `pater` (Father). The Way may therefore render the pair as `Abba, Cosmic Parent` or `Abba! Cosmic Parent!`, according to the punctuation of the passage.
- Do not introduce `Abba` into other Father passages merely as a stylistic substitute.

## Divine-name boundary
- Hebrew-first OT: preserve the source distinction among `YHWH`, `Elohim`, `El`, and other recoverable divine designations.
- Gospels / sayings-heavy NT: ordinary divine `theos` follows the project's documented Gospel convention, generally `Elohim`; explicit `pater` may be `Cosmic Parent`.
- Acts: use `Elohim` in Semitic-context lines and `God` where the documented Greek-formulaic track calls for it; explicit Father language remains Cosmic Parent.
- Pauline-and-later Greek-grounded NT: ordinary `theos` is normally `God`; explicit divine `pater` may be `Cosmic Parent`.

## Examples
- John 3:16 has Greek `theos`, not `pater`: use the project's ordinary God-language convention there, not Cosmic Parent.
- John 3:35 explicitly has `ho pater` (the Father): Cosmic Parent is appropriate there.
- Mark 14:36 has `Abba ho pater`: preserve both ideas, e.g. `Abba, Cosmic Parent`.
- Genesis 1 uses `Elohim`; Genesis 2-3 frequently uses `YHWH Elohim`. Do not replace those designations with Cosmic Parent merely to avoid a pronoun.

## QA guardrails
1. Every `Cosmic Parent` occurrence must be traceable to explicit Father/parent source language or a documented Father relationship.
2. A pronoun referring back to `YHWH`, `Elohim`, or `God` must not be expanded to Cosmic Parent unless the underlying clause itself identifies the referent as Father.
3. Preserve human father references as human kinship language.
4. Preserve all genuine `Abba` occurrences and do not manufacture new ones.
5. Audit `Cosmic Parent`, `YHWH`, `Elohim`, `God`, `Father`, and `Abba` together after corpus-wide terminology edits.
