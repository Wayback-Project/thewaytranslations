## Proposed rule

Prefer a person-centered, historically grounded, comprehensible English voice over the inherited religious vocabulary *sin / sinner / sinful*, without softening violence, exploitation, and deliberate misconduct into harmless mistakes.

**First candidate for an abstract moral noun:** `sin` → **moral failure**; `sins` → **moral failures**.  
**First candidate for a moral verb:** `sin` → **fail morally**; `sinned` → **failed morally**; `sinning` → **failing morally**.  
**First candidate for a person:** `sinner(s)` → **person / people who have failed morally**, allowing **person who does wrong** when actual harmful behavior is foregrounded.

The word **failure** alone is *not* interchangeable with the moral noun. Use **failure, failed, failings** where a genuine falling-short, omission, or unintentional mistake is established by the source and context; do not describe intentional abuse or oppression as an innocent “failure.” **Wrongdoing / wronged / acts wrongly** remain necessary for concrete injuries, betrayals, deliberate actions, and literary personification when they better convey the original. **Error, wandering, rebellion, guilt, injustice, breach** keep their distinct semantic force; do not erase an original term simply to minimize traditional vocabulary.

**Two absolute lexical exceptions:** retain the geographical **wilderness of Sin**; review `sin offering` as a technical **purification offering** when justified by ritual Hebrew `ḥaṭṭāʾt`. Neither is a moral-noun substitution.

## Source and readability tests

For each verse, determine original-language lemma, grammar/tense, actor/affected party, intentionality, literary image, and whether a full sentence becomes unnatural or misleading with `moral failure`. Record at least one serious alternative and the reason it is less suitable. Greek witnesses remain the textual basis for the NT; Semitic parallels may guide contextual interpretation without inventing an earlier Hebrew autograph. “Missing the mark” is **not** a blanket lexical definition.

## Reader compatibility

The approved canonical EPUB is the only authority for displayed Scripture. The Netlify reader builds chapter JSON, search, and EPUB hash manifests from it, rather than editing generated `/app/data/` directly. Preserve familiar *sin/sinned/sinner* queries as search aliases in metadata; never inject the traditional terms into Scripture snippets or readers.
