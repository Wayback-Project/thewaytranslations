Hebrew: adam/ha-adam can mean human, person, man; adamah = ground. Ish = man; ishah = woman/wife; ishto = his wife. Genesis 5:2 calls the male and female together adam (humankind). Havvah is named at Genesis 3:20. Unlike literal 'his wife' in Genesis 2:25 and 3:8, the approved idiomatic rendering uses 'the man and the woman' to make both persons equally visible.

Proposed Genesis references: 2:20, 2:21, 2:22, 2:25, 3:8, 3:20, 3:21, 4:1, 4:25, 5:2. Preserve 'the human' in the ground-formation narrative Genesis 2:7–19. Preserve 'your wife' and 'her husband' where marital relationship is the intended meaning, Qayin's wife in Genesis 4:17, and generic 'human heart' elsewhere.
