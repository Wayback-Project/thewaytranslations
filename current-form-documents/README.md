## What this edition represents

The Way Version is the Bible with original names brought back into view. The current edition reflects the project’s wider historical-restorative work, including:

- names such as **Yeshua, Yochanan, Ya’akov, Yosef, Maryam, Dawid**, and **Avraham**;
- distinct divine-language forms such as **YHWH, Elohim**, and **Ruach**;
- contextual treatment of terms such as *torah*, *shalom*, *chesed*, *metanoeo*, *basileia*, *Sheol*, *Hades*, and *Gehenna*;
- attention to Semitic speech, imagery, hyperbole, and oral force in the sayings of Yeshua;
- clearer contemporary English while retaining source-language texture;
- documented source distinctions rather than automatic reliance on inherited church terminology.

The edition includes many accumulated translation and editorial passes. No single recent terminology decision defines the release. Detailed reasoning belongs in `editor-notes/` and `change-logs/`, where it can be examined without turning this release page into a one-topic announcement.

## Reading and applications

The free browser reader is available now at [TheWayVersion.com/app](https://thewayversion.com/app). It is designed for phones, tablets, and desktop browsers and includes search, continuous reading, bookmarks, multi-verse highlights, notes, sharing, font controls, and reading themes.

Native mobile applications are in development. The EPUB remains the portable, offline-friendly edition for readers and e-reading applications.

## How current releases are maintained

1. Editable Scripture is maintained in `../original-documents/`.
2. Translation decisions and research are recorded in `../editor-notes/` and `../change-logs/`.
3. Before this current EPUB is replaced, the prior artifact is copied to `../rendered-documents-history/<UTC timestamp>/`.
4. The replacement EPUB is checked for container integrity, canonical book/chapter coverage, and its recorded checksum.
5. The release transaction is appended to `../rendered-documents-history/LOG.md`.

See [EPUB-REFRESH-INSTRUCTIONS.md](EPUB-REFRESH-INSTRUCTIONS.md) for the operational process and the [repository README](../README.md) for the complete project overview.
